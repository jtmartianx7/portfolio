## A Life in Design & Engineering

Five decades of work spanning motorsport, marine design, sculpture, and hands-on carpentry — from a Formula-1-chassis Ford van to a 15th-century castle roof. These pages are a visual sample from a much larger archive; a full chronological [resume](resume.md) is being assembled from it.
