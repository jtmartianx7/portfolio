## A Life in Design & Engineering

Five decades of work spanning motorsport, marine design, sculpture, and hands-on carpentry — from a Formula-1-chassis Ford van to a 15th-century castle roof. These pages are a visual sample from a much larger archive; a full chronological [resume](resume) is being assembled from it.

### Supervan 2

![Ford Supervan 2 body on a Formula-1 chassis](i/supervan.jpg)

A white commercial van body reimagined on a Cosworth F1 aluminium honeycomb chassis for Ford — "one of their more successful projects," per Ford UK.

### Motorsport & Concept Cars

![Portfolio page of race cars, dragsters, and concept vehicles](i/racecars_collection.jpg)

A career's worth of builds: a working F16 prototype, kit-car spyders, V8 Funny Cars, a jet-powered dragster, and a Ferrari P4 replica.

### Watercraft Design Sketches

![Colourful marker sketches of speedboats, jet skis, and a hovercraft](i/design_sketches.jpg)

Concept work for Designs Unlimited — outboard speedboats, a twin-engine jet ski, and a four-seater hovercraft designed while working in Singapore.

### Hydratec Speedboat

![Hydratech speedboat cockpit diagrams and engine layout](i/speedboat.jpg)

Technical detail from the Hydratec Mark III/IV — ergonomic cockpit, twin-propeller Volvo drivetrain, and the boat cutting through open water.

### Castle Roof Restoration

![Timber roof skeleton under restoration on a castle tower](i/castle_roof.jpg)

Switzerland, 2006 — replicating a 15th-century wooden roof structure. A-frames pre-built and jigsaw-assembled seven metres up.

### Static Motion Table

![Carved white sculptural table, the Static Motion Table](i/sculpture.jpg)

A sculptural coffee/dining table combining fine art with practicality — hand-carved forms paired with stained glass.

### Space-Age Concepts

![Concept art for a space-tourism simulator ride and orbital restaurant](i/restaurant_design.jpg)

A Virgin-sponsored simulator ride proposed for the Millennium Dome, and "Window View" — a dynamic Flying Restaurant concept set in orbit.
