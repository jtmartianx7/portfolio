# John's Resume

> _born 1949_
> Engineer, Artist, Visionary
> Creative Luckiness

Five decades of carpentry, metalwork, automotive and marine engineering, sculpture, and speculative design — captured piecemeal across build logs, portfolio scans, and photo albums. Dates marked **(?)** are approximate and awaiting confirmation; undated work is listed separately below in rough thematic order until it can be placed on the timeline.

## Timeline

| Year | Project | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 2006 | ![Castle roof restoration](i/castle_roof.jpg)<br>**Castle Roof Construction** — Switzerland | Replication of an original 15th-century wooden roof structure, a shade under 7 m from base to the top of the A-frame, on castle walls roughly a metre thick at the top. The roof skeleton was designed like a jigsaw puzzle — pre-built as three A-frames in the courtyard below, bottled together, then hoisted straight up through the tower and reassembled in position on the repaired wall, most likely with a twin-rotor Chinook helicopter. |
| 2002 – present | **Finca Argayall** | Ongoing carpentry, metalwork, fibreglass, painting, roadwork, and general engineering. |
| 1997 (?) | **Twin Superchargers for Aston Martin** | Details pending — to be transcribed from source material. |
| 1994 | **World Travels** | Norway, Sweden, Australia, France, England, Canary Islands. |
| 1993 (?) | **Foundation of *Designs Unlimited*** | Design studio credited across later motorsport and marine work. |
| 1992 (?) | ![Ford Supervan 2](i/supervan.jpg)<br>**Supervan 2 (Race Car Body)** — Ford | A steel-bodied Ford transit van was molded, redesigned, and reproduced as a lightweight fibreglass shell mounted on a V8 Cosworth Formula-1 aluminium honeycomb chassis. Ford called it "one of their more successful projects." |
| early 1980s | ![F16 prototype](i/racecars_collection.jpg)<br>**F16 Working Prototype Road Car** | Built for an American designer met at a car show in Geneva, where John had exhibited motorcycle pieces of his own. The kevlar-reinforced fibreglass body took about a year to build; shown at the Olympia motor show in London. |

### Motorsport

| Project | Details |
|---|---|
| ![Funny Car dragster](i/racecar_portrait.jpg)<br>**Funny Car Dragster** — UK | Built with a friend, Tony, earlier in John's career: an 8-litre, 2000 HP, supercharged and nitro-methane-injected V8 capable of 200 mph in 7 seconds. Raced successfully on UK tracks. |
| ![Rolls-Royce Orpheus dragster](i/racecars_collection.jpg)<br>**Rolls-Royce Orpheus Jet Dragster** | A customer-supplied rolling chassis fitted with a Rolls-Royce Orpheus single-spool axial-flow turbojet (developed from 1953, first flown in the prototype Folland Gnat) asked to be made "a little bit different." A Harrier jump-jet cockpit screen sourced from a scrapyard led to a wood-and-mixed-material mock-up, and finally a lightweight fibreglass shell. Both raced and widely exhibited. |
| ![Noble Spyder kit car](i/racecars_collection.jpg)<br>**Spyder Kit Car** — with Lee Noble | A minimal-parts kit car body (no doors) with a distinctive nose design. Went on to be a very successful kit car. |
| ![V8 drag cars](i/racecars_collection.jpg)<br>**Twin V8 Drag Cars** — Dodge Challenger | Two drag cars built for the same owner, raced on UK tracks. One — a heavily modified Challenger with new doors, front end, and boot lid, over 100 kg lighter — won "Best Paint" two years running. The other, its predecessor, John handled bodywork and paint only. |
| ![Ferrari P4 replica](i/racecars_collection.jpg)<br>**Ferrari P4 Replica Restoration** | Over three months, cosmetic, structural, and part modifications to bring a 1960s-era Ferrari P4 kit car into conformance with the original design. |

### Marine

| Project | Details |
|---|---|
| ![Hydratec speedboat series](i/design_sketches.jpg)<br>**Hydratec Speedboat Series** | A line of speedboat designs: the Mark II 21-foot sports car for the water (covered and uncovered variants, V8-powered Volvo Penta), the Mark III 16-foot twin-hull outboard fun boat, an outboard twin-seater monohull, and the Mark IV inline twin-hull passenger design with custom trailer. |
| ![Hydratec Part I technical drawings](i/speedboat.jpg)<br>**Hydratec Part I — Technical Design** | Detailed cockpit and engine-bay specification: handmade ergonomic steering wheel, neoprene-surfaced seating, Volvo V6 four-barrel carburettor engine, twin exhausts, and Volvo's twin-propeller system. |
| **Singapore Hovercraft** | Designed while working in Singapore: a fun 4–6-seater, twin-engined hovercraft for general public use. |
| **JetCat Twin-Engine Jet Ski** | A concept for a twin-engine jet ski, sculpted to look more exotic than the run-of-the-mill machines seen on every beach. |

|---|---|
